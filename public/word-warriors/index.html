How to play

  1. Pick 2–8 warriors. Every player gets a name, an avatar and a scoreboard spot.
  2. Choose a laga matra. Mukta, Kanna, Sihari … or the SUPER TEST with all of them mixed.
  3. Arrange the letters. Click the tiles in order — or drag them into the answer strip. Undo takes one back, Clear starts again.
  4. SUBMIT. First attempt correct = +2. Miss it and you get one more try: correct on the second = +1, otherwise 0.
  5. Pass the device. The next warrior's turn starts automatically. Scores are sorted after every round.
  6. Champion. After the last round the top three climb the podium — confetti included. 🏆
Teacher tip: every letter tile keeps its vowel sign glued to its consonant (ਵਿ stays together, never ਵ + ਿ). Open WORD BANK to add, edit or delete words — your changes are saved on this device.
